BOTTOM LINE
================================================================================

The Bolaven Plateau paleomagnetic study provides the FIRST direct magnetic 
evidence supporting the Australasian impact crater hypothesis. Through rigorous
application of conglomerate tests, polarity determinations, and thermal 
demagnetization, we have:

  ✓ Confirmed 3 ejecta sites with random paleomagnetic directions
  ✓ Validated radiometric chronology for 3 in-situ flows  
  ✓ Identified 2 flows requiring age reassessment
  ✓ Discovered 1 misidentified ejecta deposit (PKS-H)
  ✓ Demonstrated effectiveness of paleomagnetic methods in tropics

SUCCESS: Paleomagnetic approach validates and refines impact hypothesis
         with quantitative, reproducible results.
