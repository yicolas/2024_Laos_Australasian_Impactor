# Figure 1 — caption draft

**Figure 1.** Distribution of the Australasian tektite/microtektite strewn field (redrawn and updated after Folco et al., 2010, fig. 1). Red star: proposed source crater beneath the Bolaven Plateau, southern Laos (15.20°N, 106.27°E; Sieh et al., 2020). Tektites are shown by type: Muong Nong-type (layered) tektites (orange squares), splash-form tektites from SE Asia — indochinites, philippinites, javanites, billitonites and S China finds (purple stars) — and australites (black stars). Deep-sea microtektite cores (blue circles) are scaled by microtektite abundance (>125 µm per cm²; Glass, 1994; Folco et al., 2010); Antarctic microtektite sites (green diamonds) are from the Transantarctic Mountains (Folco et al., 2009, 2010, 2016; Brase et al., 2021; Chernonozhkin et al., 2021) and Dronning Maud Land (Soens et al., 2021). Dashed line: strewn-field limit; dotted lines: great-circle distance from the proposed crater. Inset: tektite localities in southern Laos and neighbouring Thailand, Vietnam and Cambodia, with the tektites analysed in this study (yellow circles); rings at 100 km intervals from the crater. Tektite localities are compiled from published geochemical datasets (Lacroix, 1935; Baker, 1956; Barnes, 1962, 1964; Taylor, 1962; Cassidy et al., 1969; Chapman & Scheiber, 1969; Chapman, 1971; Chalmers et al., 1976; Blum et al., 1992; Koeberl, 1992; Fiske et al., 1996; Son & Koeberl, 2005; Amare & Koeberl, 2006; Mizera et al., 2016; Žák et al., 2019; Tada et al., 2020) and Sieh et al. (2020, Dataset S1).
