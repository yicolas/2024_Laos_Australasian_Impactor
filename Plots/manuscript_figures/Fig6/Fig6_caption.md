# Figure 6 — caption draft (replaces LEAD_4 Figs 9 and 10)

**Figure 6.** Representative thermal demagnetization of Bolaven basalts in geographic coordinates. For each specimen, left: orthogonal-vector (Zijderveld) diagram with the horizontal-plane projection (N vs E; filled symbols) and vertical-plane projection (N vs Up; open symbols); right: remanence intensity normalised to the NRM (J/J₀) against demagnetization temperature. Least-squares line fits (principal component analysis; Kirschvink, 1980) to the low-temperature (LT, ~70–190 °C, green), mid-temperature (MT, ~210–300 °C, blue) and high-temperature (HT, ~330–510 °C, purple) components are drawn as arrows pointing in the direction of demagnetization; the HT component, which trends to the origin, is taken as the characteristic remanent magnetization (ChRM). Shaded bands on the intensity plots mark the temperature range of each fitted component. Specimen ChRM directions and maximum angular deviations (MAD) are listed above each panel. (a) NWF-A, normal-polarity in situ flow (518 ka); (b) PKS-J, a well-defined reversed direction at a site dated to the Brunhes (601 ka); (c) PKS-B (1155 ka) and (d) WBE-A (1397 ka, the basal flow), reversed in situ flows; (e, f) cores HA1 and HA2 from the upper horizon at PKS-H, 64° apart; (g) core HB1 from the lower PKS-H flow; (h) an NWF-C diamicton clast. (i) Site-mean ChRM inclination (geographic) against ⁴⁰Ar/³⁹Ar age (Table 1), with polarity intervals of the GPTS (blue normal, red reversed; Brunhes–Matuyama boundary dotted at 773 ka) and the ~790 ka impact (red dashed). Large symbols are core-level site means (Table 2), coloured by VGP polarity (blue normal, red reversed; orange the two discordant PKS-HA cores; grey diamonds the clast localities PKS-I and NWF-B); grey dots are individual specimen ChRMs, horizontal bars ±1σ of the specimen inclinations and vertical bars the 2σ age uncertainty. Dashed vertical lines mark the geocentric axial dipole inclination (±29°) at the site latitude. Letters in brackets link sites to panels a–h. Reversed flows dated to the Brunhes (PKS-K, PKS-J) are the polarity mismatches of Table 1. Most specimens lose the bulk of their remanence by ~450 °C and reach the origin by 480–510 °C, consistent with a fine-grained (titano)maghemite carrier (Fig. 4).
